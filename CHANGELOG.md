# Changelog

Each section is a version of the mod, the `version` in `mod.json`. The release workflow copies the
section of a tagged version into the release notes.

## 1.0.0

First version, from a player's suggestion.
- Elemental Brawling Knowledge (Brawlers, level 10, 5 points): the five Brawling Knowledges in one
  skill row.
- Elemental Arrows (Gunners with Kalar Species, 9 points): Fire, Ice and Electric Arrows in one.
- The price drops by what the character holds of the set, down to 1 point; buying erases those
  single ones and keeps what they had cost in the new row's purchase record; the single ones leave
  the buy list once the new skill is held.
